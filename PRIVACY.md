# Privacy Policy

**Veteran Companion**
Effective date: October 9, 2026

This policy describes how the Veteran Companion plugin (the "Plugin") handles information. The Plugin is a set of skills, and this policy applies to every skill in it. In this policy, "you" means the person using the Plugin, "Your Information" means any document, record or detail you share while using the Plugin, and "the Maintainer" means the publisher of the Plugin at https://github.com/m4ndolore/veteran-companion.

## 1. Information the Plugin reads

The Plugin helps you claim veterans' benefits and plan life after service. To do that, it reads Your Information inside your Claude conversation. Depending on the skill you use, Your Information may include:

- VA rating decisions and benefit letters;
- retirement orders, DD 214 and Physical Evaluation Board findings;
- Retiree Account Statements and other pay figures;
- service treatment records, medical exam reports and other health information; and
- denial letters and correspondence from a service board or VA.

The Plugin reads Your Information only when you share it, and only to do the task you asked for, such as drafting claim documents or estimating payments.

## 2. Information the Plugin stores

The Plugin does not store Your Information. The Plugin has no database, no log and no file storage. A skill's calculator may run on a temporary input file that contains figures such as pay, ratings and condition names. That file exists only in the session where the calculator runs.

## 3. Information the Plugin sends

The Plugin does not send Your Information to the Maintainer or to any third party. The Plugin makes no network requests. Its scripts use only the Python standard library, read local files and print their results.

## 4. Information the Maintainer receives

The Maintainer does not receive Your Information. The Maintainer may see aggregate install and usage counts that the Claude plugin directory reports. Those counts do not identify you and do not include Your Information.

## 5. Anthropic and Claude

The Plugin runs inside Claude, a service operated by Anthropic. Anthropic's handling of your conversations, including any document you upload, is governed by Anthropic's privacy policy at https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy and not by this policy. You can manage conversation history and data settings in your Claude account.

## 6. Identifiers

The Plugin instructs Claude to leave Social Security numbers and DoD ID numbers out of the documents it drafts. You add those numbers yourself when you sign the forms. Do not post drafts that contain identifiers or medical details in a public forum.

## 7. Children

The Plugin is intended for military retirees and their families. It is not intended for anyone under 18.

## 8. Changes to this policy

The Maintainer will post any change to this policy in this file and update the effective date. The history of this file in the repository shows every prior version.

## 9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted as an issue at https://github.com/m4ndolore/veteran-companion/issues.
